# Crane down (descending crane shot)

The shot starts high above the location, and the camera smoothly descends to eye level and finds the hero. First the viewer gets the place and the atmosphere, then the person or product. A classic way to open a video.

Only the camera, the effect and the action are taken from the source shot. Location, time of day, light, film look and characters are swapped in to fit the video.

## Core of the move — do not change
- Start from above: rooftops, signs, treetops, a crowd, a facade.
- A smooth vertical descent to eye level, no pans, no zoom.
- The hero enters the frame during the descent rather than being visible from the very start.
- End: a medium shot and one small action by the hero, accented with light and sound (in the original, a lighter flame on the face).
- Always keep the lines `crane down ONLY` and `No pan, no zoom, no cuts`.

## What to swap in
Film stock or look, era, time of day, weather and light, the overhead view of the location, the hero or product (with an Image 1 reference), the hero's pose and what they lean on, the final action, background audio and the sound accent.

## Prompt template
```
Shot on [FILM STOCK / LOOK], [ERA / PLACE] at [TIME OF DAY], [WEATHER], [LIGHT AND COLOR ACCENTS], [GRAIN], cinematic. Image 1 is [SUBJECT]: keep [exact face / product details]. Camera movement: crane down ONLY. The shot starts high above [ESTABLISHING VIEW: rooftops, signs, treetops, crowd] and descends smoothly to eye level, revealing [SUBJECT] [pose and place: leaning on / standing at ...] under [LIGHT SOURCE]. End: medium shot as [SUBJECT] [SMALL FINAL ACTION], [light detail on the face or product]. Audio: [AMBIENCE], [one sharp accent sound for the final action]. No pan, no zoom, no cuts.
```

## Source example
```
Shot on Kodak Vision3 500T 35mm film, 1970s Mexico City at night, rain, red and green neon signs, heavy grain, cinematic. Image 1 is Ramón: keep his exact face, mustache, slicked hair, cream guayabera and gold chain. Camera movement: crane down ONLY. The shot starts high above wet rooftops and neon signs and descends smoothly to eye level, revealing Ramón leaning on a red 1970s Ford sedan under a streetlight. End: medium shot as he lights a cigarette, the flame on his face. Audio: rain, buzzing neon, a lighter click. No pan, no zoom, no cuts.
```

## With HyperFrames
- **Live-action shot.** Generate the clip from the template in a video model and pass HyperFrames a direct https link to the mp4. The clip is inserted without sound, so request the ambience and the final sound accent in the compose prompt. Put a title or logo on the final medium shot.
- **Without generation.** In compose, describe a vertical move down a tall composition: the shot starts at the top (sky, signs, a headline), the camera travels down with a smooth ease-out and stops on the hero or product; foreground layers move faster than background layers (parallax). Works for a long vertical image or a landing page.

## Tips
- Swap the final action for something that fits the brand: the hero raises a cup, opens a laptop, wakes up a phone screen, turns a key.
- The stronger the contrast between top and bottom (cold sky, warm light around the hero), the stronger the effect.
- The move is especially good in 9:16: the vertical frame amplifies the sense of height.
- Clip length 6–10 seconds: the descent takes most of it, the final action 1–2 seconds.
